**The general Hodge Conjecture remains an open Clay Mathematics Institute Millennium Prize Problem.** 

This repository documents and formalizes the **historical timeline of verified geometric sub-cases** where the rational Hodge conjecture has been unconditionally solved by the global mathematical community.

## ⏳ Historical Timeline of Verified Sub-Cases

The mid-dimensional rational Hodge conjecture for a smooth complex projective variety \(X\) states that the rational cycle class map \(\mathrm{cl} : A^k(X)_{\mathbb{Q}} \to \mathrm{H}^{2k}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap \mathrm{H}^{k,k}(X)\) is surjective. It is unconditionally proven in the following milestones:

### 1. Codimension 1: The Lefschetz (1,1) Theorem (1924)
* **Scope:** Any smooth complex projective variety \(X\) of arbitrary dimension \(n\).
* **Result:** For \(k=1\), every rational class in \(\mathrm{H}^2(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap \mathrm{H}^{1,1}(X)\) is a rational linear combination of algebraic divisors.
* **Significance:** Serves as the unconditional foundation for all higher-dimensional reductions. If a higher-dimensional class can be pulled back into the middle cohomology of a variety of \(\dim \le 3\), it collapses into a \((1,1)\)-class and is guaranteed to be algebraic.

### 2. Uniruled Fourfolds: The Conte–Murre Theorem (1978)
* **Scope:** Smooth complex projective fourfolds (\(n=4\)) that are **uniruled**.
* **Result:** Every middle Hodge class \(\gamma \in \mathrm{H}^4(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap \mathrm{H}^{2,2}(X)\) is algebraic. 
* **Significance:** Utilizing a dominant rational map from a product space \(Y \times \mathbb{P}^1\) (where \(\dim Y = 3\)), Conte and Murre proved that the middle cohomology of a uniruled fourfold factors through the divisor space of a smooth threefold skeleton. This explicitly closed the Clay problem for this entire category of varieties.

### 3. The Diagonal Framework: Bloch–Srinivas (1983)
* **Scope:** Smooth complex projective varieties where the Chow group of 0-cycles \(\mathrm{CH}_0(X)_{\mathbb{Q}}\) is supported on a lower-dimensional subvariety.
* **Result:** Developed the **decomposition of the diagonal** correspondence method:
  \[\Delta_X = \Gamma_1 + \Gamma_2 \in \mathrm{CH}_n(X \times X)_{\mathbb{Q}}\]
* **Significance:** Provided the rigorous categorical machinery explaining *why* uniruledness tames transcendental cycles, solidifying the algebraic footprint of the intermediate skeleton mappings.
