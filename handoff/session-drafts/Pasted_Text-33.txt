Harder-Narasimhan Filtration Uniqueness in Lean 4
The Harder-Narasimhan (HN) filtration theorem states that any coherent sheaf \(E\) on a smooth projective variety possesses a unique filtration \(0 = E_0 \subset E_1 \subset \dots \subset E_n = E\) such that each factor \(G_i = E_i / E_{i-1}\) is semistable, and their slopes are strictly decreasing: \(\mu(G_1) > \mu(G_2) > \dots > \mu(G_n)\).
We formalize the uniqueness clause of this theorem in Lean 4 by stating that if two such filtrations exist, they must be isomorphic.

Wall Layout Deformations under Volume Form Changes
When the volume form or the polarization class \(\omega \) of the underlying variety deforms, the metrics defining Bridgeland stability scale unevenly. This forces the semicircular wall boundaries to warp dynamically, expanding or collapsing along the scaling parameter axis.
The script below models a sequence of volume form deformations (\(V = 1.0 \to 2.5\)) and shows how the safe stability boundaries morph across the parameter plane.
python
import numpy as np
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

# Base sheaf invariants
E = {"r": 2, "c1": 0, "ch2": -1}
F = {"r": 1, "c1": -1, "ch2": 0}

# Varying volume form deformation scales
volume_scales = [1.0, 1.4, 1.8, 2.2, 2.5]

plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))

for V in volume_scales:
    # Scale center and radius calculation based on the volume form V
    num_center = F["r"] * E["ch2"] - E["r"] * F["ch2"]
    den_center = (F["r"] * E["c1"] - E["r"] * F["c1"]) * V
    
    base_center = num_center / den_center
    # The volume element applies a geometric multiplier to the stability radius
    radius = np.sqrt(abs(base_center**2 + 2 * V * (F["ch2"]/F["r"] - E["ch2"]/E["r"])))
    
    beta_vals = np.linspace(base_center - radius + 0.01, base_center + radius - 0.01, 300)
    alpha_vals = np.sqrt(np.maximum(0, radius**2 - (beta_vals - base_center)**2))
    
    plt.plot(beta_vals, alpha_vals, label=f"Volume Form Scale $V = {V}$", linewidth=2)
    plt.fill_between(beta_vals, 0, alpha_vals, alpha=0.03)

plt.title("Wall-Crossing Loci Deformation under Volume Form Scaling", fontsize=13, fontweight='bold')
plt.xlabel(r"$\beta$ (Shift Parameter)", fontsize=11)
plt.ylabel(r"$\alpha$ (Stability Scale)", fontsize=11)
plt.xlim(-4.0, 1.0)
plt.ylim(0, 3.5)
plt.grid(True, linestyle=':', alpha=0.6)
plt.legend(loc="upper left", frameon=True)
plt.show()
