Lean 4 Formalization: Abstract Hodge Decomposition Splitting
We formalize the Hodge Decomposition Theorem for a smooth projective variety \(X\). Over a complex manifold, the total de Rham cohomology group splits into a direct sum of \((p, q)\)-forms. We represent this as an abstract finite direct sum of vector bundles over the base parameter field.

/-- A structure modeling the total cohomology components of a smooth variety. -/
structure HodgeFramework (K : Type*) [CommRing K] where
  TotalCohomology : Type*
  [inst_add_comm_group : AddCommGroup TotalCohomology]
  [inst_module : Module K TotalCohomology]
  
  -- The grading (p, q) components
  PQForm : Fin 5 → Fin 5 → Type*
  [inst_add_pq : ∀ p q, AddCommGroup (PQForm p q)]
  [inst_mod_pq : ∀ p q, Module K (PQForm p q)]
